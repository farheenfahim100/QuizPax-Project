Core functionality

Teacher Alerts

Teachers receive real-time alerts for specific questions when the accuracy rate drops below 40%, allowing teachers to understand where students are struggling.


Student Flags

Students can flag questions they find confusing without disrupting gameplay and immediately notifying the teacher.


Student Autonomy

Students answer questions independently and at their own pace, supporting autonomy and reducing performance pressure.
